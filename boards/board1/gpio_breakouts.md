# General-purpose breakouts deferred

[ADR-041](../../docs/decisions/041-timing-debug-headers.md) replaces the Rev A general GPIO breakout plan with [combined timing/debug headers](timing_headers.md). ADC, SPI/I2C and spare GPIO access will be revisited in a later revision. The prior general-breakout series-resistor proposal and its quantities are withdrawn; no associated parts or footprints are allocated.
