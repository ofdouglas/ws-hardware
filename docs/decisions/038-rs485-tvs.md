# ADR-038: RS-485 TVS selection

Accept one TI ESDS452DBZR, B1-B073, after the maintainer was informed of the +/-5.5 V working-range restriction. Both bus wires must remain within that voltage range relative to local Board 1 ground in normal operation, including remote driver voltage, ground offsets and ringing. This is the restricted ground-connected bench interface, not an assertion of full conventional RS-485 common-mode capability. Actual kit/ground-offset compliance remains unverified.

Use DBZ pins1/2 for A/B and pin3 for GND near the terminal block. Existing ST3485EBDR, bias and termination remain selected. B1-B023 excludes the new TVS row. No other TVS is fitted by this decision.

[Research](https://github.com/ofdouglas/ws-hardware/blob/dbcaba10831ade0516dba825d2c97d3f0017e0f5/boards/board1/rs485_tvs_alternatives.md) links TI SLVSHM5 pp.2–4 and DigiKey evidence. No guaranteed surge rating follows from selection: at the stated 15 A surge test the maximum clamp equals the PHY absolute limit, leaving no overshoot margin. Layout, temperature, transient coordination and footprint remain B1-Q009. No ERC/DRC or hardware tests performed.

Other [BOM closeout proposals](https://github.com/ofdouglas/ws-hardware/blob/dbcaba10831ade0516dba825d2c97d3f0017e0f5/boards/board1/bom_closeout.md) remain proposed; the user's request to suggest resolutions does not accept them.
