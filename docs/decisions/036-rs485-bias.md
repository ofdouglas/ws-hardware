# ADR-036: RS-485 idle-bus bias

Accept the recommended pair of 330 ohm 1% resistors: 3V3_SYS to ST3485EBDR A (pin6), and B (pin7) to GND. B1-B071 records quantity two, 0805; exact MPN and rating remain unselected. B1-B023 excludes these resistors and retains residual protection/control. No extra bias jumper or change to accepted termination is made.

[Bias investigation](../../boards/board1/rs485_bias.md) provides the calculation and supporting ST/TI references. Acceptance settles the network and values, not worst-case leakage/loading, cable, power-off backfeed, power budget or source-CAD qualification. B1-Q002/009 remain open. No electrical test or ERC/DRC performed.

USR-30 also requests TVS recommendations. [TVS research](https://github.com/ofdouglas/ws-hardware/blob/dbcaba10831ade0516dba825d2c97d3f0017e0f5/boards/board1/tvs_candidates.md) is proposed only; it does not accept additional MPNs or a surge rating, or replace ST3485EBDR.
